The Promises of God

God keeps His word. Every promise below is His — spoken, written, and sure.

“For all the promises of God are in him, It is; therefore also by him, amen to God, unto our glory.”

2 Corinthians 1:20

Tap, and one of His promises will be brought to you.

I

God Keeps His Word

◆

“God is not a man, that he should lie, nor as the son of man, that he should be changed. Hath he said then, and will he not do? hath he spoken, and will he not fulfill?”

Numbers 23:19

“Not so much as one word, which he had promised to perform unto them, was made void, but all came to pass.”

Josue 21:43

“So shall my word be, which shall go forth from my mouth: it shall not return to me void, but it shall do whatsoever I please, and shall prosper in the things for which I sent it.”

Isaias 55:11

“Heaven and earth shall pass, but my words shall not pass.”

Matthew 24:35

“For I am the Lord, and I change not: and you the sons of Jacob are not consumed.”

Malachias 3:6

“Let us hold fast the confession of our hope without wavering (for he is faithful that hath promised).”

Hebrews 10:23

Isaias 43:2

“The Lord is nigh unto them that are of a contrite heart: and he will save the humble of spirit.”

Psalms 33:19

“The Lord is nigh unto all them that call upon him: to all that call upon him in truth.”

Psalms 144:18

“The Lord thy God in the midst of thee is mighty, he will save: he will rejoice over thee with gladness, he will be silent in his love, he will be joyful over thee in praise.”

Osee 2:19–20

“For I am sure that neither death, nor life, nor angels, nor principalities, nor powers, nor things present, nor things to come, nor might, nor height, nor depth, nor any other creature, shall be able to separate us from the love of God, which is in Christ Jesus our Lord.”

Romans 8:38–39

No More Tears

Apocalypse 21:3–4

“He shall cast death down headlong for ever: and the Lord God shall wipe away tears from every face, and the reproach of his people he shall take away from off the whole earth: for the Lord hath spoken it.”

Isaias 25:8

“For wrath is in his indignation; and life in his good will. In the evening weeping shall have place, and in the morning gladness.”

Psalms 29:6

“They that sow in tears shall reap in joy.”

Psalms 125:5

“Blessed are they that mourn: for they shall be comforted.”

Matthew 5:5
